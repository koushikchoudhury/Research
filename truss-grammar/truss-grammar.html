    Scope

    • The rules are rebuilt from the five panels of Fig. 4 and the paper's description. The paper's own Python is in its supplementary material and was not used, so compare the two before presenting this as the paper's grammar.
    • A member is free (dashed) or solid, as drawn in Fig. 4. R1 and R3 act on free members. R5 flips only solid ones.
    • The count b = 2j − 3 assumes three support reactions. Rank comes from the rigidity matrix. The crossing, overlap and coincident-joint checks are additions beyond the paper.
    • Coordinates are unitless. No loads or material properties are modelled yet; that is the next phase.
